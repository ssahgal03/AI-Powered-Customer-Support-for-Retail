# 55-inch OLED TV

This mock model has a 55-inch OLED panel with 4K UHD resolution (3840 × 2160). Connect a soundbar to the HDMI port labeled eARC and choose HDMI eARC in the TV's sound output settings. Use the Input button to select the HDMI port used by a console or streaming device.

If the TV does not power on, unplug it for 60 seconds. Plug it directly into a wall outlet and press the physical power button on the TV. If the standby light stays off, check that the power cable is seated and try a working outlet.
