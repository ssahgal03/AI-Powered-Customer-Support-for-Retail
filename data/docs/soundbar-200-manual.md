# Soundbar 200

This mock soundbar supports HDMI ARC, optical audio, and Bluetooth. For HDMI audio, connect the soundbar's ARC port to the TV port labeled ARC or eARC. Select HDMI ARC on the soundbar and choose external speakers in the TV's audio settings. For optical audio, connect an optical cable and select Optical on the soundbar. For wireless playback, select Bluetooth and choose Soundbar 200 from the source device's pairing list.

If the soundbar powers on but has no audio, confirm the selected input matches the connection. To reset the unit, hold Power and Volume Down together for five seconds.
