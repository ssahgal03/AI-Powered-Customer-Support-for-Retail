# Wireless Headphones X

This mock headset charges over USB-C. To pair it, turn it off, then hold the power button until the indicator flashes blue. Open the device's Bluetooth settings and select Wireless Headphones X.

If pairing fails, remove the saved headset entry from the device, turn Bluetooth off and back on, and try pairing again. If the headphones will not power on, charge them with a USB-C cable before retrying.
